# Rafael Domenikos

**Mechanical engineer, computational physicist and Cambridge research alumnus working across thermodynamics, machine learning and complex systems.**

I develop mathematical models and computational methods to understand physical systems and extract structure from data. My work connects foundational questions about entropy and thermodynamics with applications in engineering, neuroscience, finance and social dynamics.

My research experience includes appointments at Nanyang Technological University (Singapore), the University of Cambridge’s Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics (UK), Stevens Institute of Technology (USA) and the National Technical University of Athens (Greece - EU), including two years as a Principal Investigator and two years as a Research Assistant Professor.

## Research directions

- **Thermodynamics and statistical physics:** entropy, maximum-entropy modelling, quantum fluids and the relationship between macroscopic behaviour and microscopic structure.
- **Machine learning and scientific computing:** predictive modelling, multimodal data analysis and computational methods informed by physical and mathematical structure.
- **Thermoinformatics and complex systems:** using thermodynamic and information-theoretic ideas to investigate biological, social and financial data.

## Selected projects

### [Generative Entropy](https://github.com/RDomenikos/Generative_Entropy)
Entropy-based modelling with applications to financial data and molecular thermodynamics, including finance and water case studies.

### [Shape-Weighted Schelling Entropy](https://github.com/RDomenikos/Shape-Weighted_Schelling_Entropy)
Agent-based simulations investigating segregation, polarization and economic constraints. The project compares Shannon entropy with shape-sensitive diagnostics and includes simulation, robustness-analysis and figure-generation workflows.

### [Thermoinformatics in Neuroscience](https://github.com/RDomenikos/Thermoinformatics_Neuroscience)
Research code connecting thermoinformational analysis with machine learning for neurophysiological systems, including EEG and optogenetic recordings.
